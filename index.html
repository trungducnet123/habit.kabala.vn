HOW IT WORKS

Not a form. Just a tap.

Most habit trackers make you fill things in: pick a date, write a note, rate how you feel. Life Touch skips all of that.

Open the app and you see a familiar grid of icons: bathroom, meds, water, coffee, meditation, exercise... Something just happened — tap that icon. The app saves the time by itself, no questions, no confirmation needed.

Want to adjust the time or add a note? You still can — it's just never required. The default is one tap, then back to whatever you were doing.
